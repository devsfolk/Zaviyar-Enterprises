Our Packaging Options

Flexible packaging solutions designed to preserve quality and meet your specific requirements

PP Bags

Durable polypropylene bags ideal for bulk shipping and storage with excellent moisture resistance.

  • Available: 5kg, 10kg, 25kg, 50kg
  • Moisture-proof and tear-resistant
  • Food-grade material certified
  • Custom branding available

Jute Bags

Eco-friendly natural fiber bags perfect for premium products and environmentally conscious buyers.

  • Available: 25kg, 50kg
  • 100% biodegradable and sustainable
  • Excellent breathability
  • Natural and rustic appearance

Container Loading

Professional container loading services ensuring secure and efficient international shipping.

  • 20ft and 40ft containers
  • Optimized space utilization
  • Proper stacking and securing
  • Inspection before sealing

Custom Packaging Solutions

  • Private Labeling: Your brand, logo, and design on every bag
  • Custom Sizes: Any weight specification from 1kg to 50kg
  • Special Materials: Vacuum-sealed, nitrogen-flushed, or multi-layer bags
  • Retail Ready: Attractive designs for direct retail shelf placement
