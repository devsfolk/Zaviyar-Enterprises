Quality Assurance

Uncompromising commitment to food safety and quality excellence at every step

Our Food Safety Commitment

At Zaviyar Enterprises, food safety is not just a requirement—it's our fundamental responsibility. We have implemented comprehensive quality management systems that ensure every grain of rice meets the highest international standards for safety, purity, and quality.

Zero Contamination

Rigorous protocols prevent any physical, chemical, or biological contamination

Full Traceability

Complete documentation from raw material sourcing to final delivery

Continuous Improvement

Regular audits and process optimization for enhanced quality

International Certifications

Certified Excellence

Our certifications demonstrate our commitment to maintaining the highest standards in food safety, quality management, and operational excellence. Each certification requires rigorous audits and continuous compliance.

HACCP Certified

Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points certification ensuring systematic prevention of food safety hazards at every critical control point in our production process.

ISO Certification

International standard demonstrating our quality management system's effectiveness in consistently meeting customer and regulatory requirements through documented processes and procedures.

Food Safety Standards

Full compliance with international food safety regulations including proper sanitation, hygiene practices, and documentation ensuring safe products for global markets.

Hygienic Processing Environment

Our facility maintains the highest standards of cleanliness and hygiene throughout all processing areas. From controlled access zones to regular sanitation schedules, every aspect is designed to prevent contamination.

Facility Controls

  • Temperature-controlled processing and storage areas
  • HEPA filtration systems for air quality control
  • Restricted access with proper gowning procedures
  • Regular equipment sanitization and maintenance
  • Pest control monitoring and prevention programs

Personnel Hygiene

  • Mandatory food safety training for all employees
  • Protective clothing and hair nets in all areas
  • Hand washing stations at all entry and exit points
  • Regular health screenings and hygiene audits
  • No jewelry, cosmetics, or personal items policy

Comprehensive Laboratory Testing

Advanced Testing Protocols

Our state-of-the-art laboratory conducts comprehensive testing on every batch to ensure compliance with international quality standards. No product leaves our facility without passing rigorous quality checks.

Physical Analysis

Grain length, width, broken percentage, whiteness index, and color uniformity measurements

Chemical Testing

Moisture content, protein levels, starch composition, and pesticide residue analysis

Microbiological Testing

Bacterial count, mold detection, and pathogen screening for food safety compliance

Cooking Quality

Elongation ratio, aroma retention, texture, and taste evaluation
