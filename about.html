About Zaviyar Enterprises

Excellence in rice milling since 2008 – Where tradition meets innovation

Our Journey

Since our establishment in 2008, Zaviyar Enterprises has been at the forefront of rice milling excellence. What began as a vision to provide premium quality rice has evolved into a state-of-the-art operation serving global markets with unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction.

Over the years, we have invested heavily in modern infrastructure, advanced technology, and skilled personnel to ensure that every grain of rice we process meets the highest international standards. Our journey is marked by continuous improvement, innovation, and an uncompromising focus on food safety.

Today, Zaviyar Enterprises stands as a trusted name in the rice milling industry, recognized for our premium Basmati and Non-Basmati varieties, exceptional quality control, and reliable global export services.

Our Vision

To be the leading rice supplier globally, recognized for premium quality, sustainable practices, and customer-centric service that sets the benchmark for excellence in the rice milling industry.

Our Mission

To deliver superior rice products through advanced milling technology, rigorous quality control, and adherence to international food safety standards, while building lasting relationships with our global partners.

Quality Certifications

HACCP Certified

Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points certification ensuring food safety at every production stage. Our HACCP system identifies, evaluates, and controls hazards significant to food safety.

ISO Certified

International Organization for Standardization certification demonstrating our commitment to quality management systems, continuous improvement, and meeting customer and regulatory requirements consistently.

Food Safety Standards

Compliance with international food safety standards including proper documentation, traceability, and hygienic processing practices that ensure consumer safety and product integrity.

Export Compliance

Full compliance with international export regulations, phytosanitary requirements, and importing country specifications ensuring smooth customs clearance and product acceptance worldwide.
